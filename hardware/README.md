# Hardware

The Fritzing `.fzz` files are circuit-design artifacts recovered from the project archive. Open them with Fritzing and validate pin mappings against the corresponding firmware before building hardware.

`BOM.csv` records the hardware inventory that can be reconstructed from the experimental documentation. It intentionally leaves contemporaneous prices blank because a formal bill-of-materials cost study was not performed and original purchase prices were not preserved. The manuscript therefore treats the prototype as low-cost qualitatively rather than assigning a reconstructed per-node cost.
